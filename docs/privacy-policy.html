MathCrossword Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

MathCrossword is an offline mathematical puzzle application. This policy describes how the app handles gameplay and research data.

Developer / publisher

MathCrossword is an independent project published by the developer identified on the app's Google Play store listing. Privacy questions may be submitted through the public MathCrossword GitHub repository issue tracker.

Data stored on the device

The app stores gameplay progress and local session telemetry on the user's device. Session telemetry may include puzzle identifiers and seeds, app and generator versions, puzzle structure metrics, timestamps and active-time measurements, placements, candidate-note actions, undo actions, hints, navigation events, solver-route comparisons, and derived statistics used to evaluate puzzle difficulty and generation quality.

This information is used locally to provide progress, analysis, difficulty calibration, and research-oriented diagnostics inside the app.

Automatic collection and transmission

The Google Play distribution does not automatically upload gameplay or research telemetry to the developer or to a third-party analytics service. The current Google Play build does not require network access for gameplay and does not contain advertising or account sign-in.

Research export

The app can create a research export only after the user explicitly chooses the export action. The archive may contain metadata.json, sessions.jsonl, and summary.json. Metadata includes a randomly generated installation identifier so that multiple exports from the same installation can be grouped without using a Google account, advertising identifier, phone number, contacts, or location.

After creation, Android lets the user choose where to save or share the archive. The app does not choose the recipient and does not automatically send the archive to the developer. If the user voluntarily sends an export to another person or service, that recipient's handling of the file is outside the app's control.

Personal and sensitive information

MathCrossword does not intentionally request or collect a user's name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, payment information, photos, microphone recordings, or health information.

Accounts and advertising

The app has no user accounts, no login, no advertising, and no advertising SDK.

Retention and deletion

Gameplay progress and research telemetry remain in the app's local storage until they are removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. A research archive created by the user is a separate file and remains wherever the user saved or shared it until the user or recipient deletes it.

Security

Local telemetry is kept in the app's private storage. The app does not operate a background telemetry-upload service in the current Google Play distribution.

Children

MathCrossword is a general mathematical puzzle application. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. The target-audience declaration in Google Play must remain consistent with the actual store listing and future app features.

Future changes

If a future version adds optional research synchronization, accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or another form of off-device data transmission, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that behavior is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the MathCrossword repository at GitHub Issues. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
